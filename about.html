LATIMO is a Northern Cape (Kathu) based cleaning and supplying company which started off in 2012, to provide good quality cleaning and supplying services and sustainable cleaning solutions for mining plants and industrial sectors.

We have over 6 years of experience in the industrial cleaning and supplying services to a wide range of clients, we pride ourselves to provide only high quality services.

Quality of service and the commitment of the director and staff members have ensured that our customer retention rate is among the highest in the cleaning industry.

We continuously strive for Zero Harm to our people and our environment and are committed to improving the quality of life of people and communities in and around our operations.
