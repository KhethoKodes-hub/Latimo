Who We Are

About LATIMO

LATIMO is a Northern Cape (Kathu) based cleaning and supplying company which started in 2012, providing exceptional quality cleaning and sustainable solutions for mining plants and industrial sectors.

With over 6 years of experience, we've built our reputation on the quality of service and the unwavering commitment of our director and staff — resulting in one of the highest customer retention rates in the cleaning industry.
